I run on pure delusion.

          
I'm Mushaf, a software engineer and digital artist in London. Pure delusion is how I get through everything: whatever is in front of me, I decide it works out before there's any reason to, then move like it already has. People ask how I stay so calm. That's how.

          

            
"So the delusion was never blind, just a choice to believe."

          

          

            

              
HyperFixation

              
I call it HyperFixation. When something grabs me, whether it's software, a painting or a video edit, I dive into the deep end and stay there until hours turn into days. My final year game took well over 300 hours, with days where I was dialled in for 14 to 20 hours straight, and some paintings take 20. I count it as my greatest ability.

            

            

              
Blame Jarvis

              
I grew up watching Tony Stark talk to Jarvis and never got over it. Whenever something new lands in AI, I'm testing it the same day, pushing it until it breaks and building with it by the weekend. Noctis, the system that runs my PCs, is that childhood idea made real.
