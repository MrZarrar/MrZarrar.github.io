Noctis OS

          
My own control plane for AI agents

        

        
        

          
I talk to Noctis from my phone or my desk. It runs Claude Code and Codex across my home and work PCs, remembers what matters from my notes, opens live panels while it works, and stops to ask me before anything spends money or leaves the house. Noctis Editor

          
A video editor Claude can cut with you

        

        
        

          
Ask for an edit in plain words and Claude makes it on the real timeline, then keep adjusting the same timeline by hand. Both of you share one undo history, and motion graphics stay editable as HTML and GSAP instead of being baked into pixels. The film here was edited in it.

          

            
Built with
Rust, TypeScript, Python

In active development

glycolens

          
Pattern finding for my Type 1 diabetes

        

        
        

          
Every week it pulls fourteen days of data from my t:slim X2 pump and Dexcom G7, checks my carb ratio, correction factor, basal and pre-bolus timing with fixed rules, and suggests one small, safe change. Lows always win, and no step is bigger than ten percent. Noctis runs it every Sunday. It's built for me alone and isn't medical advice.

          

            
Built with
Python, PowerShell

            
Runs
Weekly, from my home PC
